I am a doctoral researcher at Aalto University, Finland, supervised by Prof. Haoye Tian. Previously, I obtained my M.Eng. degree in Computer Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology under the supervision of Prof. Yuxia Zhang, where I also received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology. My research focused on the sustainability of open source software (OSS) ecosystems, in particular commercial participation in OSS. This work has been published at ICSE, FSE, TSE, and TOSEM. Before starting my Ph.D., I worked as a backend development engineer at Alibaba.
